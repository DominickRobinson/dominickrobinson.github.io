The goal of the project was to investigate whether the time of a course affected student performance. I designing a blocking experiment to isolate the effect of course time on performance by designing the experiment to optimally control for instructor, student year, race, gender, and prior GPA. This project consisted of the following steps:

1. Designing the experiment
2. Writing the [proposal](/pdf/S26/627_proposal.pdf)
3. Performing the analysis
4. Writing the [report](/pdf/S26/627_report.pdf)
