I led this game in GCS during Fall 2022. I was inspired by [Angry Bunnies: Colossal Carrot Crusade](https://www.nintendo.com/us/store/products/angry-bunnies-colossal-carrot-crusade-switch/?srsltid=AfmBOorMp3t3hTBJpgJDmiu0yEcM23LAx7uibSJp8mVNe0Ybnpya45bo) (hence the name). I initially had a very intensive semester lined up, but after dropping [15-213: Intro to Computer Systems](https://www.cs.cmu.edu/~213/), I suddenly had a ton of time to sink into the development of this game. 

I learned so much about game development while tinkering with this project. The scope became very large, but I felt that I managed it well with a clear roadmap. For fun, I took screenshots of each level and the text I wrote for each level/cutscene, and I created a book out of it.
