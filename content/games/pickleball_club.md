I created this in my Comedy Writing class during Spring 2024. The game is based on the history of CMU's Pickleball Club, which I founded. The game features around 50 levels, 10,000+ words, and 600+ AI-generated images. As of Janurary 2026, this is my favorite game that I have developed so far. I feel that it's the funniest thing I've ever made (especially if you know the club's history), and I am proud that the game seems kind of fun to play. It is also the first game I have published on the iOS app store.
