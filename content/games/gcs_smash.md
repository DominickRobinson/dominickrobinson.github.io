I led this game in GCS in Fall 2024. I was inspired by GCS's long history featuring so many interested and loved characters, as well as my fondness and reverence for Super Smash Bros., which many regard as the "Hall of Fame" of video game characters. I forked an existing Godot Super Smash Bros. clone.
