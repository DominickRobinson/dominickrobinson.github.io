I developed the math theoretic game of Cops and Robbers in Godot as a [SURA](https://www.cmu.edu/uro/summer%20research%20fellowships/sura/index.html) project under [Professor David Offner](https://www.cmu.edu/math/people/faculty/offner.html). I implemented various features, such as variants of the game, a graph creator, some level packs, and an auto-level generator. This is definitely my most technically advanced game I've made so far. I intended to remake and expand upon this game in the future...

[Play on itch.io](https://domonation-studios.itch.io/cops-and-robbers)
