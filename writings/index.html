America’s Great Depression

Spring 2022
While not the funniest thing I’ve ever written, this piece is why I pursued a Creative Writing minor. My professor enjoyed the humor of my work, my transformation of a typical college essay into entertainment, and suggested that I pursue a Creative Writing minor, so I looked into it and thought “why not?”

This paper was written for a required first-year writing course called “Writing about Public Problems,” and I decided to write about a mundane local issue instead of a greater societal issue that I would not have had any real authority over. My mom enjoyed this work.
